Karabo's revenue design

Two separate income streams - not one.

To maximise the revenue stream we sell the bot to people and monetise the trading volume those clients generate. Seat fees alone leave money on the table. Volume rebate / IB income is the second engine.

Stream 1 - Sell the bot

  • Access seats into the ~1000-trader network
  • R16,000 access fee + 10% of positive winnings
  • Clients keep their own capital on their own accounts
  • Commercial revenue splits 50/50

This is the upfront / recurring product business.

Stream 2 - Monetise volume

  • Cut an affiliate / IB deal with a broker
  • Bring them clients who use Axe Edge on that venue
  • Earn a cut of the spread on their trading volume
  • Going rate: about $3-$90 per lot (often higher with unregulated brokers)

This pays while clients keep trading - separate from seat fees.

Why this structure wins

Seat sales fund the launch. Broker / IB rebates compound as the book grows. Two streams means you are not dependent on one billing cycle. Revenue can fund personal trading capital, ops, or whatever you both agree to allocate - after the 50/50 split.

Note: affiliate payouts depend on the broker contract, regulated vs unregulated terms, and whether clients actually trade on the referred account. We lock the IB partner and rebate share in writing before public push.
