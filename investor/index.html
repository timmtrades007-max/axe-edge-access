How we trade

The operating model

This is mechanical BTCUSDT trading on Bybit-style perpetual structure. The chart is there so you can see entries and exits resolve in market time - not to sell a headline number.

1. Signal → limit

When the system arms a trade, it places a limit entry. We wait for price. If it does not fill, there is no chase and no market order panic.

2. Bracket the risk

Each fill carries a take-profit and a stop. Wins and losses are decided by those levels - that is what the live win rate counts.

3. Manage the tape

Open risk is visible on the chart as entry / TP / SL while a trade is live. Host placeholder closes are excluded from the quality readout.

4. Compound the book

As equity builds, order size increases. Early tickets stay small; later tickets are larger. The equity panel is that compounding in motion.

5. What you are watching

The 15m chart is a sped-up tape of the verified path - candles print and scroll the way a live chart does on fast-forward, with major BTC events marked.
