1. Market structure - not predictions

Entries are driven by candle structure and confluence on BTCUSDT perpetual markets. The system waits for conditions to align instead of forcing trades because "it feels right."

2. Confirmation before capital is at risk

Axe Edge uses multi-condition confirmation. If the tape does not clear the checklist, it stands down. Fewer forced trades. Cleaner risk.

3. Regime awareness

Strong trending / dangerous tape is treated differently from quiet structure. The system is built to avoid fighting the market when conditions are hostile, and to participate when the setup quality is high.

4. Realistic fill thinking

Proof runs use actual Bybit OHLCV + mark data and a soft-touch fill model - not fantasy "perfect limit every time" assumptions. Live fills can still differ. That honesty is part of the sell.

5. Compounding explains the curve

Start capital is modest. Over thousands of trades, winners recycle into size. Even with a hard size ceiling, a long verified window can still compound meaningfully - without inventing untradeable millions at first glance. The engine is process + time + leverage discipline - not a lottery ticket.

6. What we never claim

We do not promise future profits, identical live fills, or risk-free trading. Leverage can amplify losses. Drawdowns and liquidations are possible. Past backtests are evidence of process - not a guarantee.
